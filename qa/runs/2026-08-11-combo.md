The badge is present and correctly labelled everywhere it is meant to be. **Runbook row corrected** from 5 paths to 4, with the exclusion explained so future runs stop hunting for it.

## Caveat the reviewer wants on record

**A and B were not walked this run**, and 2.4.0 introduces a new capability (`use_mvs_documents`) whose whole risk profile is *upgrade* behaviour — an existing site must not lose its drives. That specific concern was verified directly rather than by a full upgrade walk: `mvs_caps_version` was cleared to force the version-gated re-grant, after which every core role plus a freshly registered custom role held the capability, an owner's revocation survived a subsequent `add_caps()`, and a revoke/restore cycle left all 11 of the member's documents intact. That is the substance of the B_upgrade concern for this change, but it is not a substitute for a clean 2.3.x → 2.4.0 install-and-upgrade run, which remains the honest gap before tagging.
