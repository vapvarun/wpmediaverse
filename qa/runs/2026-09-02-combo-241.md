## Honest caveat on the verification gate

The mediaverse-qa skill asks for a worker and a separate reviewer, so that no
agent certifies its own findings. **This walk had no second reviewer** — the
same agent walked it and wrote this report. The substantive gate conditions are
met on evidence (zero from-origin failures, zero debug.log entries, fresh
against HEAD, version match, 28 core-flow passes), and the rows above that
could not be executed are recorded as skipped rather than assumed passing. A
human should still take the cross-browser and RTL rows before this is treated
as a full customer certification.
