## Findings

F1. Editor can delete another member's album/collection over REST (step 1a).
- Repro: as an Editor, DELETE /wp-json/mvs/v1/albums/{id} and /collections/{id} of a member's album.
- Expected (brief): 403. Actual: 204, row deleted (not trashed). PUT is 403 as expected; wp-admin list/edit/new 403.
- Cause: AlbumController.php:695 delete check uses moderate_mvs_media; CollectionController.php:519 same. Editor role is granted moderate_mvs_media by Free. Brief item (d) says moderators may take down but not edit, which is exactly this; the conflict is the brief, not necessarily the code. Owner decision needed: is a default Editor a moderator?
- Origin: from 2.6.4 (v2.6.3..2.6.4 changed delete from delete_others_mvs_medias / owner_permissions_check to moderate_mvs_media).
