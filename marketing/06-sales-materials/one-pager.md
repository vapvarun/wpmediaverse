*For use in sales conversations, plugin directory listings, partner pitches, and anywhere a quick overview is needed.*

## The Problem

WordPress communities rely on BuddyPress activity feeds to share media. Photos scroll past and disappear. Albums are basic. There is no competition engine, no cloud storage routing, no per-user quota, and moderation is entirely manual.

The established plugins in this space — rtMedia, MediaPress — were built years ago and have not kept pace. The UI is dated, extensibility is limited, and modern requirements like cloud storage, video tooling, and gamification are either missing or bolted on awkwardly.

Community admins are running photo competitions in spreadsheets. Education admins are watching storage quotas blow out. Developers are hacking template files to get basic customisations done.

---

## The Solution

WPMediaVerse is a dedicated media platform for WordPress communities. It sits alongside your existing BuddyPress setup (or works standalone) and gives members a proper home for photos and videos — with the tools community admins, developers, educators, and club owners actually need.

Built on custom database tables. Documented REST API. BuddyPress integration as a separate, optional layer.

---

## Five Things WPMediaVerse Does That Others Do Not

### 1. AI Moderation Built Into the Free Version
Every upload passes through an AI moderation check before it appears in the feed. Flagged content goes into a review queue — you see it before your members do. No additional plugin, no separate service setup needed.

### 2. Photo Battles, Challenges, and Tournaments (Pro)
Run head-to-head photo battles with community voting. Create theme-based challenges with submission deadlines and automatic leaderboards. Run bracket-style tournaments with points awarded at every round. This is built into Pro — not a third-party integration.

### 3. Cloud Storage with S3 and BunnyCDN (Pro)
Route uploaded media directly to Amazon S3 or BunnyCDN. Keeps your hosting server clean, reduces costs on high-volume sites, and makes media delivery faster for your members worldwide.

### 4. Video Chapters, Resume Playback, and Captions (Pro)
Members upload MP4s and WPMediaVerse plays them as uploaded — no encoding queue, no FFmpeg on your server. Pro adds chapter markers, resume playback that reopens a long video where the member left it, auto-captions via Whisper, and retention heatmaps. No relying on YouTube or Vimeo.

### 5. Per-User Storage Quotas with MemberPress and WooCommerce (Pro)
Set storage limits per role or membership tier. Integrate directly with MemberPress or WooCommerce — no custom code needed. When a member hits their limit, they see a clear upgrade prompt. Works out of the box for education platforms and tiered membership sites.
