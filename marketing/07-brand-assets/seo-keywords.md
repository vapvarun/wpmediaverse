# WPMediaVerse SEO Keyword Reference

Organised by search intent. Use this to plan blog posts, landing pages, and on-page optimisation. Priority ratings are relative to each other within a group, not cross-group comparisons.

---

## How to Use This Document

- **Informational keywords** — target with blog posts, how-to guides, comparison articles
- **Commercial keywords** — target with feature landing pages, comparison pages, use-case pages
- **Transactional keywords** — target with the product page, pricing page, checkout flow copy

Volume estimates are approximate. Prioritise based on fit first, volume second.

## Keywords to Avoid Targeting (Now)

These have high competition relative to the current domain authority, or are too broad to convert well from organic traffic.

- "wordpress gallery plugin" — dominated by Envira, Modula, NextGEN
- "best wordpress plugin" — impossible to rank
- "wordpress media library" — refers to WP core, intent mismatch
- "social network wordpress" — BuddyBoss and BuddyPress own this, too broad
- "wordpress video plugin" — very broad, dominated by video embed tools not relevant to this product
