The single document view

A member opens their own contract and cannot tell how big it is, whether it is private, or where it lives. Every one of those facts is already in the database. This is what the page should show instead.

Three defects were fixed today by looking at the rendered page rather than the DOM — a duplicate Share label, actions stacked 60 px apart, and a caveat set as body copy. What is left is not a bug list. It is that the page was built to display a preview, and a document needs to display a document: its identity, its state, and what you may do with it.

The one that matters

Privacy is invisible. A member looking at a private contract sees exactly what they would see looking at a public one. The page offers a Share access button that can mint a link anyone can open — and gives no indication of the document's current state before they press it. That is a confidentiality decision made without the facts.

The four changes

  • 1

    A metadata line under the title. Type chip, size, privacy, location.

    The drive row already shows all four; the single page shows none, so moving from the list to the detail view loses information. The type chip reuses the drive's existing vocabulary rather than inventing a second one.

  • 2

    Privacy as a chip, not a word in a sentence.

    State that changes what sharing means should be readable at a glance, in form as well as text. Public documents show no chip — absence is the quiet default, and only the restricted states earn a marker.

  • 3

    One primary action. Download leads; Follow steps back to a ghost button.

    Today Follow and Download are both solid red at opposite corners, so the loudest thing on a document page is a social action about its author. Following a person is not why anyone opened this page.

  • 4

    One inset for every block.

    Measured on the live page: the document block sits at x = 396 and the reactions bar at x = 412. Sixteen pixels of drift inside a single card is the kind of thing nobody names and everybody feels.

Notes for building it

Where the markup goes

The metadata line belongs to Free, in templates/media-single.php beside the title — it is composed entirely of values Free already holds. Only location needs Pro, since folders are a Pro concept, so it renders through a filter that Free leaves empty and Pro answers. That keeps a Free-only site from showing a blank slot where a folder name would be.

Absence is a state

A document at the drive root shows no location fragment rather than the words “Drive root”; a public document shows no privacy chip. Only conditions worth acting on get a marker, so a page full of chips means something is genuinely unusual.

Do not add a second social row

The reactions and Favourite/Save/Share/Report bars come from Free's shared media surface and are correct as they are. The fix there is alignment, not new controls.

Sequencing

Worth doing before this: the Share access button currently offers only an anonymous public link, while the drive offers sharing with a member or a role. Adding a privacy chip beside a button whose only option is “anyone with the link” would sharpen the contradiction rather than resolve it.
